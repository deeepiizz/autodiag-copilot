# Catalytic Converter and Oxygen Sensors

> Illustrative procedure for the demo vehicle fleet. Not an OEM service manual.

## Upstream vs downstream sensors
Sensor 1 sits before the catalyst and switches rapidly between rich and lean; the ECM uses it for fuel control. Sensor 2 sits after the catalyst. With a healthy catalyst the downstream signal is steady because the converter stores oxygen.

## Catalyst efficiency (P0420)
P0420 sets when the downstream sensor starts switching like the upstream one, meaning the catalyst no longer stores oxygen. Before replacing the converter, fix any misfire or fuel-trim fault, check for exhaust leaks near the downstream sensor, and confirm the downstream sensor responds correctly.

## O2 sensor response tests
A healthy upstream sensor switches between about 0.1 V and 0.9 V several times per second at 2500 rpm. A slow sensor (P0133) shows lazy transitions. A sensor stuck low (P0131) can be caused by an exhaust leak pulling in outside air.

## Heater circuit faults (P0135)
Measure heater resistance at the sensor connector; an open circuit means the sensor must be replaced. Check the heater fuse and power supply before condemning the sensor.
