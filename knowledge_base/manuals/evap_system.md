# Evaporative Emission (EVAP) System

> Illustrative procedure for the demo vehicle fleet. Not an OEM service manual.

## System overview
The EVAP system stores fuel vapour in a charcoal canister and purges it into the engine. The ECM runs leak tests by sealing the system with the vent valve and watching tank pressure.

## Leak codes
P0455 indicates a large leak, P0442 a small leak and P0456 a very small leak. The most common cause of all three is the fuel cap: check that it clicks closed and that its seal is not cracked. Clear the code and allow several drive cycles for the monitor to re-run.

## Smoke testing
Connect a smoke machine to the EVAP service port and close the vent valve with a scan tool. Look for smoke at hoses, the filler neck, the purge valve and the canister.

## Purge and vent valves
A purge valve stuck open can cause a rough idle after refuelling. A vent valve stuck open prevents the system from sealing and sets a large-leak code.
