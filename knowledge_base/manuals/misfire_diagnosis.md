# Engine Misfire Diagnosis

> Illustrative procedure for the demo vehicle fleet. Not an OEM service manual.

## Symptoms
A misfire shows up as rough idle, hesitation under load, a flashing check-engine lamp and codes P0300 to P0304. A flashing MIL means the misfire is severe enough to overheat the catalytic converter, so the vehicle should not be driven hard.

## Isolating the cylinder
Read the per-cylinder misfire counters with a scan tool. If one cylinder dominates, swap its ignition coil with a neighbouring cylinder and clear the codes. If the misfire follows the coil, replace the coil. If it stays, swap the spark plug next, then test the injector.

## Spark plug inspection
Remove the plugs and compare them. A wet, fuel-fouled plug points to an injector or ignition fault on that cylinder. Worn electrodes or a gap above specification cause misfires under load. Replace plugs as a set.

## Random misfire (P0300)
When misfires are spread across cylinders, look for a shared cause: a vacuum leak, low fuel pressure, a dirty MAF sensor or a weak ignition supply. Check long-term fuel trims; trims above +10% at idle suggest a lean condition feeding the misfire.

## Compression and mechanical checks
If ignition and fuel are good, run a compression test. A cylinder more than 15% below the others indicates valve or ring damage. A leak-down test separates intake valve, exhaust valve and ring leakage.
