# 12 V Charging System

> Illustrative procedure for the demo vehicle fleet. Not an OEM service manual.

## System voltage low (P0562)
The ECM sets P0562 when it sees system voltage below its threshold for a set time. Low voltage can also trigger unrelated communication codes as modules brown out.

## Battery test
With the engine off and the battery rested, about 12.6 V means fully charged and below 12.2 V means discharged. Load-test or conductance-test the battery before blaming the alternator.

## Alternator output
With the engine running, voltage at the battery should be about 13.8 to 14.7 V. Below 13.2 V under load points to a failing alternator, a slipping belt or a poor connection. Measure voltage drop across the positive and ground cables while the engine is cranking: more than 0.5 V means high resistance.
